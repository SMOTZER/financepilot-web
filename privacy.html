FinancePilot privacy policy

FinancePilot is a personal budgeting app used by its owner.

Where your data lives. Everything you enter stays in your browser or phone. If you connect Google Drive, the app saves a copy to a FinancePilot folder in your own Drive. No other server receives your data.

Google access. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only files it created. It cannot read anything else in your Drive. It doesn't share, sell or analyze your data.

Removing access. Disconnect in the app's Settings, or remove FinancePilot at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Delete the FinancePilot folder in Drive to remove the stored copies.
